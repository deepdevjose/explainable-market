# Code of conduct

Treat contributors respectfully. Critique ideas and code, not people. Welcome
questions and differences in experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
sexualized conduct and disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate content and restrict participation.
Report conduct issues privately through an available repository-maintainer contact;
do not publish private information while requesting help. Reports will be handled
with care and shared only as needed to address the concern. Maintainers must follow
the same standards and avoid retaliation.
